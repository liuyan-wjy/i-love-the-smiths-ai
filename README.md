# I Love the Smiths AI

Turn one or two photos into a 20-second elevator scene inspired by the familiar “I love the Smiths” exchange from *(500) Days of Summer*.

**[Visit the website →](https://ilovethesmithsai.net/)**

## What you can make

Upload a photo, choose your role, and let the scene play out. With one photo, a fictional AI co-star takes the other role. With two photos, you can put both people in the scene. The dialogue, music, and timing are already set, so there is no prompt to write.

Each completed generation includes:

- A 20-second landscape video in 16:9, delivered as a 720p MP4 with sound and no watermark.
- Two 9:16 versions for vertical feeds: a blurred-background layout and a stacked layout. These may finish a few minutes after the main video.

## How to use it

1. Open [ilovethesmithsai.net](https://ilovethesmithsai.net/).
2. Upload one or two clear photos of consenting adults.
3. Choose who wears the headphones and who speaks first.
4. Complete checkout and follow the generation on your order page.
5. Download your video when it is ready. An email also lets you return to the order.

No account or subscription is required. Current pricing and delivery estimates are listed on the website.

## A few things to know

Use only photos you have permission to upload. AI-generated details such as hair and clothing can vary; the result is an interpretation rather than an exact copy of a photograph.

I Love the Smiths AI is an independent project and is not affiliated with the film or the band. This repository contains the product overview and usage guide.
